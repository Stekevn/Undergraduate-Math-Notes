# Undergraduate Math Notes

This repository contains lecture notes and formal proofs I authored for student-led mathematics seminars during the Winter of 2026. 

## Contents

### 1. Number Theory
* **Reference**: *A Course in Arithmetic* by J.-P. Serre (Chapters 1–3)
* **Topics Covered**: Detailed proofs of the Law of Quadratic Reciprocity and the properties of the Hilbert symbol.

### 2. Algebraic Topology
* **Reference**: *Algebraic Topology* by Allen Hatcher (Chapter 1)
* **Topics Covered**: The formal proof of the Seifert-van Kampen Theorem and its structural applications.
